## Claim

Staleness penalty grows super-linearly with corpus update rate: below 100 blocks/day the frozen model degrades negligibly (< 2% accuracy drop per week), but above 1000 blocks/day staleness becomes the dominant error source within 48 hours.

## Operationalization

Dataset: a corpus with a controllable update stream (synthetic amendments to a contract corpus).
Metric: task accuracy on a stable held-out eval set, measured daily for 14 days after export.
Baseline: frozen artifact exported on day 0.
Intervention: vary update rate (10 / 100 / 1000 / 10K blocks/day injected into live graph). Re-run eval daily without re-exporting the frozen artifact.
Acceptance criterion: super-linear relationship is confirmed if accuracy decay rate increases by > 3x when moving from 100 to 1000 blocks/day.

## Null Hypothesis

Staleness penalty scales linearly with update rate; the 100 blocks/day vs. 1000 blocks/day boundary is not a meaningful inflection point — any update rate causes proportional degradation.

## Experiment Spec

1. Export a frozen GGUF artifact from the live graph on day 0.
2. Run a controlled update stream at 4 rates: 10, 100, 1000, 10K blocks/day. Each update stream introduces semantically meaningful changes (contract amendments, policy updates) — not random noise.
3. Evaluate the frozen artifact and the live graph-resident client daily on the same 200-question held-out set.
4. Plot accuracy decay curves for all 4 update rates.
5. Fit a curve to identify the inflection point and confirm or deny super-linearity.

## Results

### Area 7 integrated full run (issue #18)

Simulated decay over 14 days at 4 update rates using
`experiments/area7-differentiable-graph/staleness_curve.py`. The simulator
seeds initial accuracy from the trained-model contradiction-detection score
(0.898) and applies a deterministic exponential decay parameterised by
update-rate-induced corpus drift. This is a simulation, not a live
re-evaluation against an updating corpus — the live decay benchmark is
deferred to the workload-level run.

| Update rate (blocks/day) | Half-life (days) | Day-14 accuracy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | ≥14 (no measurable decay) | 0.897 |
| 100 | ≥14 (no measurable decay) | 0.893 |
| 1,000 | ≥14 | 0.849 |
| 10,000 | 3.37 | 0.666 |

Super-linear inflection appears between 1K and 10K blocks/day in this
simulation (half-life collapses from ≥14 days to 3.37 days). The 100→1000
ratio criterion (≥3x decay-rate increase) is not met by the simulation
alone — the decay rate is essentially flat in that band. H7.3 is therefore
recorded as partially-supported pending a live workload measurement.
