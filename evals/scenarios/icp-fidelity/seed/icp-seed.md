# Seed intent: keep our vendor onboarding checklist from falling through the cracks

I run operations for a 60-person distribution company. We don't have any
full-time engineers — I'm the closest thing to "IT" we've got, and mostly I
just keep the laptops updated and argue with the phone system.

Right now, when we bring on a new vendor, someone has to remember to: collect
their W-9, check their insurance certificate is current, get them set up in
the accounting system, and confirm a manager signed off before we cut them
their first PO. It's all tracked in a shared spreadsheet and a pile of emails,
and twice this year we paid a vendor before their insurance paperwork actually
cleared, which our insurance broker was not thrilled about.

What I want: a small internal tool where someone can add a new vendor, check
off each onboarding step as it's done, and see at a glance who's still
missing something. Nothing fancy — I don't need integrations with our
accounting software or anything like that yet, just a single source of truth
so we stop finding out about gaps after the fact.

The one thing I do care about: I don't want this thing making changes on its
own. Whatever it sets up for us, I want to see what it's proposing and give
it an explicit thumbs-up before anything goes live. I've read enough
"autonomous AI" horror stories to know I want a human — me — in the loop
until I trust it.
