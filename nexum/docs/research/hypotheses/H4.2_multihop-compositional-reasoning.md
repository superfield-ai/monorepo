## Claim

Multi-step compositional questions ("does clause A in contract X override clause B in contract Y given law Z?") require ≥3-hop graph traversal; Nexum-style typed-link traversal matches or beats vanilla similarity-only RAG accuracy at every hop depth ≥3.

## Operationalization

Dataset: 100 synthetic legal-style questions across hop depths 2/3/4/5 (25 per bucket) over a 30-contract corpus with explicit cross-document reference chains. CUAD does not annotate cross-document references, so a synthetic corpus is the only honest substrate at this stage.
Metric: answer accuracy = does the gold span (a token unique to the answer block) appear in the union of retrieved text?
Baseline: top-k=8 cosine retrieval over paragraph blocks (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, 384-d).
Intervention: same top-k=8 cosine seed retrieval, then BFS traversal of typed cross-document links up to `n_hops` steps.
Acceptance criterion: graph_accuracy > vanilla_accuracy at every measured hop depth ≥3.

## Null Hypothesis

Vanilla similarity retrieval finds the answer block at all hop depths because seed and answer documents share enough surface tokens; the graph traversal contributes nothing measurable.

## Experiment Spec

1. Build a synthetic corpus of 30 contracts, each with 6 paragraph blocks corresponding to standard clause kinds. Wire deterministic cross-document references (B0→B0 of next contracts) so each block-0 chain is at least 5 hops long.
2. For each hop depth ∈ {2,3,4,5}, generate 25 questions that name only the seed contract and require following N typed-link hops to reach the answer-bearing block.
3. Run two retrievers on the same questions with the same embedder: vanilla (top-k cosine) and graph (top-k cosine seed + BFS typed-link traversal).
4. Score: gold-span substring containment in the union of retrieved text.
5. Report per-hop accuracy and the graph-vs-vanilla delta curve.

## Results

**Run (2026-05-10, synthetic corpus, 100 questions, all-MiniLM-L6-v2):**

| Hops | Vanilla acc | Graph acc | Delta |
| ---: | ----------: | --------: | ----: |
| 2 | 0.200 | 0.640 | +0.440 |
| 3 | 0.200 | 0.680 | +0.480 |
| 4 | 0.280 | 0.920 | +0.640 |
| 5 | 0.200 | 0.800 | +0.600 |

**Status: PASSED** (synthetic-corpus ceiling). Graph traversal beats vanilla at every measured hop depth ≥2, and the absolute delta widens with hop count (+0.44 at 2-hop → +0.64 at 4-hop). Vanilla accuracy is bounded by whether the answer-bearing block happens to be inside the seed retrieval's top-k window; for 4-hop chains across 30 contracts that probability is ~28%, while graph traversal lifts it to 92%.

This is a *capability* result, not a *naturalness* one. It demonstrates that **if** a corpus carries cross-document reference structure, Nexum's typed-link layer recovers multi-hop answers that pure cosine retrieval cannot. It does not measure how often real legal corpora carry such structure — that is the open question for future CourtListener / EDGAR runs.
